Since 1953

A Legend
Forged Underwater

Launched in 1953, the Submariner was the first divers' watch to be waterproof to a depth of 100 metres. Seventy years later, it remains the definitive reference in aquatic timekeeping — water-resistant to 300 metres.

Its Oystersteel case and unidirectional rotatable bezel with Cerachrom insert in black ceramic have become symbols of exploration trusted by professionals and connoisseurs alike.

Rolex Technology

Engineered for
Extremes

Cerachrom Bezel
Virtually scratch-proof ceramic insert moulded and fired at over 1500°C. Its colour remains stable even under intense UV exposure — guaranteed for generations.
Oyster Architecture
The Oyster case — invented by Rolex in 1926 — forms a hermetic capsule protecting the movement. The Triplock winding crown seals the case to 300 metres.
Chromalight Lume
Long-lasting blue luminescent material applied to hour markers and Mercedes hands. Emits light twice as long as standard luminescent material in complete darkness.
